# Comprehensive Help Center

## Goal
Turn `/faq` into a searchable-feeling, categorized help center while preserving Onlooker LLC’s established dark neon styling and legal language.

## Changes
- Add five horizontal filter tabs: Bounties & Payouts, Emergency & Community Reporting, Live Streaming & Media, Real Estate & Verification, and Account, Safety & Compliance.
- Populate each category with focused, collapsible FAQ answers covering platform workflow, escrow and the two-hour review window, the 85/15 payout split, reporting trust levels, streaming/media rules, the six-digit real-estate verification process, account safety, Terms, Privacy, and copyright/DMCA.
- Keep only the selected category’s accordion visible so the page stays concise on mobile.
- Add a prominent circular X in the header that returns to the exact previous page, with Home as a direct-entry fallback.
- Retain support and legal links at the bottom.

## Validation
- Check mobile and desktop layouts, category switching, accordion expansion, and the close-button return behavior.
- Confirm the page loads without browser errors and has complete page metadata.
